Why Legerly

The information
gap is real.

When you use an AI product, the provider knows what it costs to serve you and how much of your plan you really use. You're left guessing, and that gap makes it easy to pay for more than you need. Our tools hand that information back.

01

Hidden costs

Token pricing is buried in documentation. Real-time cost during a conversation? Nowhere to be found. We show it to you as you type.

02

Opaque subscriptions

$20 a month sounds reasonable — but is it, for how you use AI? Without your own numbers you're guessing. We give you the numbers and do the maths.

03

Vague limits

Plans promise "5× more usage" without saying 5× of what. We show what each plan's limits are known to be, and say plainly where the provider doesn't disclose them.

04

No neutral comparison

Every model comparison is written by someone selling something. We're not. Legerly tools are ad-free and have no affiliate relationships.
