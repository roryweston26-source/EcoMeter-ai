The Transparency Index is retired.

It graded how openly AI companies disclose their environmental footprint, pricing and data practices. As of November 2026 Legerly only does one job: helping you pay the right amount for the AI you actually use. The Index's grades were last checked in September 2026 and are no longer kept up to date, so they are not published here any more.

One part lives on. If you tell the Subscription Auditor that privacy matters most, it still says what each provider discloses about training on your chats, and links to the provider's own page for each point.

  • Subscription AuditorTell it how you use AI and get the plan that fits, or the reason you don't need one.
  • AI Price TrackerEvery model's per-token rate, and how much use a subscription needs before it beats paying per token.
  • EcoMeter AIA browser extension that shows what each conversation costs as you go, and exports your real usage to the Auditor.
