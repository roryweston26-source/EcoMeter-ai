The AI Clock is retired.

It was a modelled projection of global AI use, not a measurement, and it didn't help anyone decide anything. As of November 2026 Legerly only does one job: helping you pay the right amount for the AI you actually use.

  • AI Price TrackerEvery model's per-token rate, and how much use a subscription needs before it beats paying per token.
  • EcoMeter AIA browser extension that shows what each conversation costs as you go, and exports your real usage to the Auditor.
